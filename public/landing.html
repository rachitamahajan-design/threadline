No bot joins your call. Nothing leaves your laptop.

Notes with receipts

Every claim points at the line where it was said.

Decisions, risks and action items each carry a receipt — a timestamped quote from the call. A claim with no proof never ships: a gate blocks it and records the reason.

Meeting modes

Tell it what room you're in. The notes change shape.

Investor, vendor, customer, team or 1:1 — the mode picks the sections the notes reach for, and which handoff gets suggested after. An investor call surfaces traction and asks; a 1:1 surfaces signals and growth. Same meeting brain, different lens.
